Netflix lets you download a list of everything you have played, with the date attached. Mine covers about two and a half years. It does not come with any explanation, so I made a few charts to see what was in it.

Two years, day by day

Each square is one day, and the darker squares are days I played more. Blank squares are days I did not watch anything.

2024 is mostly blank — plays, with long gaps between them.

2025 fills in. is the busiest month in the file at plays, outlined here.

2026 covers only January through August, plays so far.

Across all three years the plays arrive in stretches rather than a little every day.

Mostly K-drama

One genre accounts for most of the hours. Each play counts once, under a single primary genre, so a show with several tags does not get counted twice.

K-drama fills most of the months. Grey grows when I was in the middle of an English series or an anime, and then K-drama comes back.

The shows I came back to

Counting episodes favors shows with short ones. A cooking competition with hour-long episodes adds up faster than a half-hour sitcom, so this list is ranked by hours instead.

Sex and the City is still first. After that the list mixes anime, a Korean cooking competition, K-dramas, and Bridgerton — old rewatches and recent finds in the same top eight.

Sundays, mostly

I usually spend Sunday at home watching something, so I expected Sunday to be the busiest day of the week.

It is, by a clear margin. Saturday is close behind, and Monday is the lowest.
